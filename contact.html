COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You Get in Touch.

A few helpful answers about contacting our team.

How can I schedule a consultation?

Visit our Booking page, choose the consultation details and submit your request. Appointment confirmation depends on the booking system being connected and reviewed.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Include your contact details, area of interest, a short subject and a clear description of your requirements. Avoid sharing confidential documents through the initial contact form.

Can businesses contact LEXORA for consulting?

Yes. Businesses can use the enquiry form to ask about strategy, risk management, operational consulting and other advisory requirements.

Does submitting an enquiry create a confirmed appointment?

No. An enquiry is only an initial communication. A consultation is confirmed only after the appointment details have been reviewed and confirmation has been provided.
